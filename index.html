FEB 21 2024
Presidential Inspection

President Ferdinand Marcos, Jr. leads inspection of Airport–New Clark City Access Road (ANAR)

President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. on Wednesday, February 21 led the inspection of the 19.81-kilometer, six-lane Airport–New Clark City Access Road (ANAR), which will cut travel time from other districts of Clark to New Clark City to about 20 minutes from an hour, toll free.

The ANAR project is a key infrastructure initiative connecting Clark International Airport to New Clark City, facilitating smoother movement of passengers, cargo, and residents across the region — a milestone that AICC is proud to have contributed to.

DEC 18 2023
Community Event

RunRio Season Finale at the Iconic Sacobia Bridge and NCC Spur Road

RUNRIO'S SEASON FINALE. What a way to finish 2023 with the staging of the inaugural New Clark City Half Marathon by Runrio Inc., fueled by Gatorade Philippines.

Both seasoned and recreational runners traversed the 21-kilometer route from the iconic Sacobia Bridge in Clark Freeport Zone all the way to the world-class New Clark City Athletics Stadium, via Airport to New Clark City Access Road (ANAR). The race also featured 10k and 5k categories.

FEB 23 2021
Groundbreaking Ceremony

Groundbreaking for Relocation and Replication of ASCOM Facilities

Officials from Bases Conversion and Development Authority, Department of National Defense and Armed Forces of the Philippines hold a groundbreaking ceremony for the construction of Army Support Command (ASCOM) facilities in Camp Servillano Aquino, Tarlac.

The ceremony marked the official start of AICC's scope of work — the relocation and replication of critical ASCOM infrastructure, reinforcing AICC's reputation as a trusted partner for government and defense projects.
